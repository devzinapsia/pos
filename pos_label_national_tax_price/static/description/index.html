POS Label National Tax Price

Adds a new product label format, Etiqueta para comandera con precio, to the standard "Print Labels" wizard, sized for 80mm-wide thermal ticket printers: every requested label stacked on one continuous 7.2cm-wide page instead of one small page per label.

It prints the product name (no barcode) and final (tax included) price, plus a new legend showing the price with Argentina's national taxes (VAT and national indirect taxes) excluded — computed by reusing the same tax classification already used by the AR localization's invoice/ticket "Fiscal Transparency" (RG 5614/2024) breakdown.

Available to every company in the database.
