POS Label National Tax Price

Adds a new product label format, National tax price (7.2 x 4 cm), to the standard "Print Labels" wizard, sized for 80mm-wide thermal ticket printers.

Derived from the standard Odoo Dymo label report, it prints the product name, barcode and final (tax included) price, plus a new legend showing the price with Argentina's national taxes (VAT and national indirect taxes) excluded — computed by reusing the same tax classification already used by the AR localization's invoice/ticket "Fiscal Transparency" (RG 5614/2024) breakdown.

Available to every company in the database.
